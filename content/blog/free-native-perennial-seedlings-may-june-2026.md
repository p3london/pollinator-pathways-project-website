Until June 5, 2026, you can request up to 24 native perennial seedlings from Pollinator Pathways Project (P3). There will be 3 types of seedlings available:

* [Anise Hyssop](https://www.thespruce.com/anise-hyssop-plant-profile-5118429)
* [Black Eyed Susan](https://www.thespruce.com/choosing-and-growing-black-eyed-susan-1402860)
* [Bee Balm / Wild Bergamot](https://www.thespruce.com/bee-balm-growing-guide-5204121)

The seedlings are currently being grown by P3 volunteers, and will be available for pickup on Saturday, June 6th, from a London location. P3 will contact you by email on Saturday, May 30 to advise you of the location and time for the pickup on the following Saturday, June 6.

To apply for your free seedlings, please send an email to [pollinatorpathwaysproject@gmail.com](mailto:pollinatorpathwaysproject@gmail.com?subject=Free%20Seedlings) with the subject line “Free Seedlings”, and provide your name and email address.
